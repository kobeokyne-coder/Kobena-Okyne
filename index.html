// Recovery Intel

Recover Like a Pro

The gains happen during recovery, not the workout. These aren't optional extras — they're part of the program.

😴 Sleep 8–9 Hours

Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. Prioritize sleep hygiene — same bedtime, dark room, no screens 30 min before bed.

🧊 Cold Therapy

Cold showers or ice baths post-workout reduce inflammation, boost dopamine, and accelerate muscle recovery. 2–5 min.

🧘 Active Mobility

Daily 10–15 min of stretching, yoga, or foam rolling prevents injury and keeps the body supple and ready to perform.

💊 Key Supplements

Creatine (5g/day), Vitamin D3, Magnesium, Omega-3s, and Protein powder. Supplements assist — they don't replace food.

📵 Mental Recovery

Stress is the enemy of gains. Meditate, journal, or decompress daily. Mental fitness is part of a disciplined lifestyle.

📈 Track & Adjust

Log workouts, meals, and sleep weekly. What gets measured gets improved. Data is your best training partner.

// Creator Profile

The Mind Behind FitCore

Health Science Student · Saint Joseph's University · Philadelphia, PA

I'm a Health Science student at Saint Joseph's University, rooted in Upper Darby, PA — a community that shaped my understanding of resilience, work ethic, and the power of representation. My dad is from Ghana, my mom from Liberia — two proud West African nations whose cultures of strength and community run through everything I do.

FitCore isn't just a fitness site. It's a mission. I created it because I believe health is a right, not a privilege — and that too many people don't see themselves represented in wellness spaces. I'm here to change that. Whether you're a first-generation student trying to balance nutrition and classes, or anyone who's been told the gym isn't for them — this is built for you.

I'm driven by a simple belief: discipline is the bridge between your goals and your results. And I want to walk that bridge with as many people as possible.
